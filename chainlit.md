# AI Team

Chat with Specialist 1, Specialist 2, Evaluator, Executor, and Technical.

The specialists research a plan from official sources, question each other, and
fact-check until they agree. Specialist 1 asks you questions only when both
specialists agree they need your input. Evaluator then fact-checks that plan
and fixes major issues. Executor is the project manager: validation, accuracy,
and keeping the thread aligned. Once Executor signs off, Technical codes and
builds what the plan asks for.

Attach images or spreadsheets with the paperclip; they can send files back in
the same thread. Past chats are in the left sidebar.
